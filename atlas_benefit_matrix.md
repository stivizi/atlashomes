# Atlas Modular vs. Traditional Building: Benefit Matrix

This matrix highlights the key advantages of Atlas Modular's high-performance, light-steel frame construction compared to traditional wood-frame building methods.

| Feature / Aspect          | Atlas Modular (Light-Steel Frame)                     | Traditional Building (Wood Frame)                  |
| :------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------ |
| **Framing Material**      | CNC Light-Steel (Inorganic)                           | Lumber (Organic)                                  |
| **Precision**             | High (0.5mm tolerance via CNC)                        | Lower (Manual framing, subject to variation)      |
| **Durability / Lifespan** | Extremely High (Projected centuries)                  | Limited (Susceptible to elements, decades)        |
| **Resistance to Elements**| Excellent (No rot, mold, warping, pests)              | Poor (Susceptible to rot, mold, warping, pests)   |
| **Fire Safety**           | Superior (Non-combustible/flame-spread resistant)     | Poor (Combustible material)                       |
| **Construction Speed**    | Significantly Faster (Up to 50% reduction)            | Slower (Sequential on-site process)               |
| **Quality Control**       | High (Strict CSA factory standards, controlled env.)  | Variable (On-site conditions, weather dependent)  |
| **Waste Generation**      | Minimal (Up to 90% reduction, factory recycling)      | High (Significant on-site waste)                  |
| **Site Disruption**       | Low (Less time, noise, traffic on site)               | High (Extended on-site activity)                  |
| **Cost Certainty**        | High (Components specified & priced upfront)          | Lower (Subject to on-site delays, material costs) |
| **Labour Efficiency**     | High (Optimized factory processes)                    | Lower (Reliant on skilled on-site trades)         |
| **Environmental Impact**  | Lower (Less waste, durable materials, efficiency)     | Higher (More waste, shorter lifespan materials)   |
| **Structural Integrity**  | Consistently Straight, Square, Plumb                  | Can vary, potential for settling/warping          |
