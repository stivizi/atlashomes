# Atlas Modular: Building the Future, Today.

## High-Performance Modular Homes: Stronger, Faster, Smarter.

Atlas Modular Solutions, leveraging industry expertise since 2006, transitioned from premier installation services to manufacturing, recognizing the limitations of traditional wood framing. Wood rots, swells, molds, and warps. Atlas builds differently, constructing the highest quality modular buildings using a superior light-steel framing system for unparalleled longevity and performance.

---

### Why Atlas Modular?

*   **Superior Framing:** Built with the Framecad light-steel system, every stud is CNC printed to a 0.5mm tolerance. Perfectly straight, square, and plumb, with a projected lifespan of multiple centuries.
*   **High Performance:** Constructed with inorganic, non-combustible or certified flame-spread resistant materials. In the event of a fire, Atlas buildings resist ignition and flame spread.
*   **Sustainability & Safety:** Engineered for longevity, durability, and environmental responsibility. Built under strict CSA A660 (steel structures) and CSA A277/Z241 (buildings) standards.
*   **Unmatched Efficiency:** Experience significantly faster build times, drastically reduced construction waste, and minimal disruption to the site and community.
